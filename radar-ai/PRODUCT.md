# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Product Marketing Managers, Strategists, and Founders / Executive Leadership who need to track competitors and understand market movements.

## Product Purpose
Senro.AI is an AI-powered competitive intelligence platform. It monitors competitor websites, detects meaningful changes (pricing, features, positioning), and uses persistent AI memory to connect these events over time. Success means providing users with strategic insights and patterns rather than just isolated alerts.

## Positioning
Unlike traditional competitive tracking tools that only show what changed (simple website diffs), Senro.AI remembers past changes and uses AI to explain *why* the change matters, identifying broader strategic patterns over time.

## Operating Context
Users view dashboards and receive alerts to monitor competitor activity without manual checking. They use these insights to make strategic decisions, adjust pricing, or update their own product positioning.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Tracks competitor websites for updates (pricing, products, landing pages, CTAs, messaging).
- AI analysis extracts meaningful signals beyond simple text differences.
- Persistent memory stores past observations to contextualize new ones.
- Provides a dashboard and alert system.
- Tech Stack: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, Vercel.

## Brand Commitments
Clean, modern, minimalist B2B SaaS aesthetic.
